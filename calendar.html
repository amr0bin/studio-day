The opportunities that fit, and what each one needs

Grants, prizes, opens and residencies for figurative, portrait and still-life work in acrylic and pencil, open to a Canadian resident. Every entry carries a source and the date it was read. Anything projected from a previous cycle is marked expected and has to be confirmed when the call opens.

The readiness kit

What every entry below asks for, prepared once and refreshed quarterly, so that an application is assembly rather than production.

Rolling, or undated

    How this page is kept

    Dates are read on the organisation's own page and stamped with the day they were read. A projected date is a placeholder for planning and is labelled expected until the call is posted. Eligibility, medium and whether physical work must travel before selection are recorded per entry, because those three things decide whether an opportunity costs an evening or a month.

    Apply municipally and provincially before federally, because a grant record makes every subsequent application read differently. Enter the digital-first prizes freely; enter the ship-before-selection ones only when a specific work justifies the freight.
