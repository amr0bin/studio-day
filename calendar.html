CalendarA rolling window, inside a ten-year ladder

The opportunities that fit, and what each one needs

Grants, prizes, opens and residencies for figurative, portrait and still-life work in acrylic and pencil, open to a Canadian resident. Every entry carries a source and the date it was read. Anything projected from a previous cycle is marked expected and has to be confirmed when the call opens. The dated window runs about eighteen months ahead and is rewritten each November. What the window is climbing toward is the ladder below it.

The readiness kit

What every entry below asks for, prepared once and refreshed quarterly, so that an application is assembly rather than production.

Rolling, or undated

    The ten-year ladder

    There are no dates on this section, and there will not be. A deadline eight years out is a guess, and a reference page holding stale figures is worse than one holding none, because stale figures get trusted. What can be known now is the order, and the order is the useful part: each rung is applied for from the record the rung below it built.

    Each rung deposits into one of the five ledgers. The funded record is the obvious one; the relational record is the one that decides whether the higher rungs are ever reachable, and it is built years before it is needed.

    How this page is kept

    Dates are read on the organisation's own page and stamped with the day they were read. A projected date is a placeholder for planning and is labelled expected until the call is posted. Eligibility, medium and whether physical work must travel before selection are recorded per entry, because those three things decide whether an opportunity costs an evening or a month.

    Apply municipally and provincially before federally, because a grant record makes every subsequent application read differently. Enter the digital-first prizes freely; enter the ship-before-selection ones only when a specific work justifies the freight.

    The dated window is rewritten each November, when the year’s catalogue is closed. Entries that have passed are moved to the record rather than deleted, because a list of what was entered is itself evidence of a professional practice and several funders ask for exactly that.
