## Decisions recorded, all made 11 November 2026

1. **Size:** 9 × 12 inches for studies and prints.
2. **Pencil studies:** $600, on archival 100% cotton paper.
3. **Paint studies:** $800, on archival 100% cotton paper.
4. **Limited archival prints:** $250 each, no more than 30 in a batch.
5. **Greeting card packs:** $45 for six, with 10% going to Canuck Place's arts
   program. The percentage goes up once releases reliably sell out.

Replaces $350 on paper and $450 on linen (24 October), and prints at $125 at 8 × 10.

## Print cost estimate, added 11 November 2026

9 × 12 prints: $31 each for 1–4, $27 for 5–20, $25 for 21 or more. The floor keeps
the earlier non-print costs (finishing kit, time, overhead, about $53) and swaps in
the new print cost: about $78 at 21+, $80 at 5–20, $84 at 1–4. At $250 a print sells
for a little over three times its floor. Ordering a full batch of 21–30 after a
pre-order closes gets the $25 rate.
