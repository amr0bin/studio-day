# Cards and Prints in Shops: One-Year Preparation Plan

*Written 19 Nov 2026, fitted to Studio Day (github.com/amr0bin/studio-day). Target: cards on shelves by November 2027. Living document; update as decisions change.*

## The ceiling

By November 2027, a finished card line sits in Old Faithful Shop, Paper-Ya, and Kimprints, with a Whole Foods application backed by those stockists and a collection under review or signed at The Poster Club. Every card carries your name, your site, and the Canuck Place donation. The shops then sell through December without needing you, which is exactly what the wintering month asks for.

## How this fits Studio Day

- **Sunday stays for making.** Nothing in this plan touches it.
- **Monday evening carries the work.** One Monday block a month goes to this plan; the checklists below are sized for that.
- **The Monday letter does the pitching.** Shop owners and buyers become the "one named person" on the weeks you choose.
- **December stays clear.** The plan starts in January 2027 and has stock delivered before December 2027.
- **Autumn is when selling happens**, so pitches land in late August and September. The Year gives the September to November Monday blocks to the book, so in-person pitching happens in shop hours, not in the Monday block.
- **Lanes:** scanning and card design sit in Make, cards on shelves sit in Share, and shop owners sit in Relationships.
- **Income ranking:** Studio Day ranks retail last by paid hours returned. Licensing returns the most per hour here, since The Poster Club would print, sell, and ship. Wholesale is the hours-hungry half, so it is capped at one restock Monday a month.

## Already settled (11 November 2026, in the repo)

- Wholesale price: $22.50 a pack of six, half of the $45 retail price.
- Minimum order: 6 packs ($135).
- Wholesale floor: about $6 a pack, hand-delivered.
- Donation: 10% of what you receive, about $2.25 a pack, to Canuck Place's arts program.
- Terms: invoiced on delivery, net 30.
- Scope: only shops with a strong relationship (East Van Roasters, Old Faithful Shop, Granville Island).

## What today's conversation adds or changes

- **Scope widens.** Whole Foods, Paper-Ya, Kimprints, and other Gastown shops go beyond "shops with a strong relationship". Either the rule changes, or the relationship is built first through the Monday letter.
- **Singles.** Shops mostly sell cards one at a time. Decide whether shops may split packs, or whether you supply sleeved singles (which would be $3.75 each at the same half-of-retail rate).
- **Barcodes and insurance.** Whole Foods validates barcodes against GS1 and lists liability insurance as a requirement.
- **Licensing is a new income type**, not yet in the pricing references or the income ranking.
- **Sitter permissions.** The sitter agreement and image-use release offer tick boxes for the annual book, catalogues, and journals, but none for prints or cards for sale, or for licensing an image to a third party.

## Two deals, kept separate

| | Licensing (The Poster Club) | Wholesale (cards, your own prints) |
|---|---|---|
| Who makes it | They do | You do |
| What you earn | Royalty or fee, per their contract | $22.50 a pack, less about $6 floor and the donation |
| Rights | You license named images | You grant nothing |
| Your ongoing work | Almost none | Printing, packing, invoicing, and restocking |

Rule: two image pools. One set made for licensing, one for your own cards and limited editions. No image sits in both.

## The year

### December 2026: wintering

Nothing scheduled.

### January to March 2027: decide and design

- [ ] Walk Granville Island and Gastown as a customer. Record each shop's card prices, sizes, paper, packaging, and racks.
- [ ] Choose the first 8 to 12 card images and mark which pool each belongs to.
- [ ] Add two tick boxes to the sitter agreement and image-use release: prints and cards for sale, and licensing to a third party. Use only images that are clear on both.
- [ ] Decide packs only, or packs and singles.
- [ ] Design the card back: name, title of the work, andrearobinstudio.com, the Canuck Place line, and space for a barcode.

Scanning each study at 600 dpi continues inside the existing Monday documentation protocol; those files are the masters for cards and prints.

### April to June 2027: sample and set up

- [ ] Print a proof run and check colour against the originals.
- [ ] Join GS1 Canada for a company prefix and assign a barcode to each design.
- [ ] Get quotes for commercial liability insurance.
- [ ] Ask the accountant about GST/HST in the same email as the year-end books (registration becomes mandatory above $30,000 in annual revenue; confirm the current rule).
- [ ] Draft the one-page line sheet from the settled terms.
- [ ] Join or consult CARFAC BC so a licence contract can be reviewed quickly when one arrives.
- [ ] Email the Poster Club submission to submissions@theposterclub.com: personal information, a short introduction, and a selection of work. Reply time is not published, so early is better.

The Leeds MSc application is also due in late spring; the list above is kept short for that reason.

### July and August 2027: stock and first contact

- [ ] Photograph the cards properly, singly and as a set.
- [ ] Print opening stock deep enough to fill three shops and one reorder each.
- [ ] Register on the Whole Foods Supplier Portal and ask at your store who the local forager for BC is now.
- [ ] Monday letters to Old Faithful, Kimprints, and Paper-Ya: the line is coming; how and when do they review new work?
- [ ] Late August: Old Faithful in person, with samples and the line sheet.

Everything that needs setting up is finished by the end of August, before a September MSc start and the book months.

### September and October 2027: pitch

- [ ] Kimprints and Paper-Ya.
- [ ] One Granville Island afternoon: Blue Seas Gallery, Rhinoceros, Niche, The Postcard Place.
- [ ] One Gastown afternoon: Out & About, Under the Stoop, Shop Makers.
- [ ] Submit the Whole Foods Potential Supplier Form, naming current stockists.

Shops usually choose holiday cards in late summer and early autumn, so these pitches can still catch the 2027 season.

### November 2027: close the book

- [ ] Deliver a full restock to every stockist before December.
- [ ] Year-end review: which designs sold, which shops reordered, and what the hours cost.
- [ ] Decide the 2028 line and whether prints follow the cards into the same shops.

### December 2027: wintering

Shops sell; nothing needs fulfilling.

## Ready-to-pitch checklist

- [ ] 8 to 12 designs printed, sleeved, and barcoded
- [ ] Sitter permissions cover every image used
- [ ] Line sheet carrying the settled terms
- [ ] Opening stock on hand
- [ ] Liability insurance in place
- [ ] Decision on exclusivity between neighbouring shops
- [ ] Image pools recorded in the Notion catalogue

## Open decisions

- Does the "no selling until the threshold" rule cover wholesale, or does shop placement count as visibility?
- Does wholesale stay limited to shops with a strong relationship?
- Packs only, or singles too?
- Which images go to licensing and which stay yours
- Where licensing sits in the ranking of income by hours returned
- Whether any shop gets exclusive designs
- Whether someone else packs and delivers restocks once three shops are stocked

## Not yet verified

- The Poster Club's royalty, exclusivity, and contract length
- Whether BC Whole Foods stores stock local artist cards, and who the forager is
- Each shop's buying process and terms (none publish one)
- Shop Makers' shelf-rental terms

## Fallback, named once

If a shop says no, ask what would change the answer, and pitch the next one on the list. The preparation is the same for all of them.
