# Supervisors

*The actual gate at every practice-based and art history door is a person who already knows the work. This file exists so that the relationship predates the application by years rather than months.*

**The rule:** a first approach is never an application. It is a short letter with one piece of writing attached and a specific question about their work. The ask comes later, or not at all.

**The rate:** one new academic contact a year, which over a decade is enough.
