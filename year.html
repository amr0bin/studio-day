The YearSeptember 2026 to December 2027

Sixteen months, three lanes

What is made, what is shared with collectors and learners, and which relationships get a next step, month by month across the actual span rather than an abstract year. Two things run every week regardless: one Patreon piece, written from Sunday's sentence, and one named person written to on Monday. Select a month; the dated card is drawn from the Calendar and updates with it. Solid dots are confirmed dates, hollow ones expected.

Capacity holds two of making, video and selling admin. Not three. Anything new waits until the thing before it is easy.

The rhythm underneath

The lanes above are this span. The wheel below is the shape any year takes: three months make, two prepare, two teach, four gather, one is kept empty. When the lanes and the wheel disagree, the wheel is the rule and the lanes are the exception that has to justify itself.

Select a month on the wheel. Each opens what the month is for, what runs and what pauses, the milestone that has to be met before it ends, and how the Monday blocks are spent.

Why this stands

How the year is held

The year has one fixed deadline and one protected month. The book is completed in November. December is wintering.

December holds a birthday and the anniversary of a significant family loss, and it is kept as a retreat with no commitments to anyone else wherever that is possible. The November deadline exists so that December is genuinely clear. That means no launches, no live sessions, and no drops requiring fulfilment in December. Anything already recorded can keep selling on its own. Nothing should need her.

This runs against the commercial grain, since December is a strong month for art sales. The decision stands anyway.

Why summer holds the teaching

The Foundations module is six weeks at three hours a week, taught live and recorded as it goes. Delivery is eighteen hours and preparation is another twelve to eighteen, so the block is roughly a month of total available time compressed into six weeks. It has to displace something, and the studies series is the thing that gives.

Summer delivery gives spring to prepare and autumn to sell the recording. It also keeps the six-week block clear of coursework once the MSc begins, which is the constraint that will matter most from the second year onward.

Why the book is cheap to make

The writing already exists, committed to the archive week by week. The documentation happens in the Monday blocks regardless of whether a book is being made. Photographing work at completion rather than gathering it at the end is what turns November into an assembly month instead of a scramble.

Compilation is a selection and sequencing task. It belongs in Monday blocks through October and November rather than in Sunday studio time, and nothing new is made for it.

The leverage

One year of work produces four outputs: the paintings, the studies series, the course, and the book. The course footage comes from sessions taught live rather than filmed separately. The book comes from writing and photographs that already exist. Neither is a new production project.
