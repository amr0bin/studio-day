## I. CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY

This curriculum is structured on the shape of a rigorous Master of Fine Arts (MFA) degree [688]. It runs concurrently with a professional learning design career, ensuring that high-level studio practice, critical theory, and academic inquiry are fully integrated into daily life.

### 1. The Nine Core Courses [688, 690]
Every single week of this 5-year curriculum coordinates nine distinct lines of active practice. No component runs in isolation; they continuously feed, scaffold, and test one another:
1.  **Studio I (Open Studio Production)**: The bedrock of volume. Daily 20-minute weekday sessions + Sunday open studio hours [690].
2.  **Studio II (The Portfolio Course)**: Staged, milestone-driven assignments with strict technical and conceptual constraints [690, 479].
3.  **Technique (The Foundations Drill)**: A rigorous 16-module technical sequence covering Stage 1 (Seeing & Thinking), Stage 2 (Form & Structure), Stage 3 (Colour & Composition), and Stage 4 (Voice & Expression) [691].
4.  **Critique (Fiduciary Feedback)**: Faculty-led monthly reviews with your critic, combined with Granville Island Peer Critiques [690].
5.  **Seminar (History & Criticism)**: Core theoretical readings read with a pencil, combined with monthly Vancouver gallery and artist talks [690].
6.  **Exhibition (The Proposal Road)**: Proposal-based, competitive entries written for real-world galleries and artist-run spaces [690, 804].
7.  **Visiting Artists (Inverted Residency)**: Direct outreach and quarterly interviews with professional painters for *Dear Ordinary* [690].
8.  **Field School (Geographical Displacement)**: Annual deep-dives away with painting tools to challenge spatial habits [690].
9.  **Thesis (The Body as the Site of the Sacred)**: Compilation of 56 pieces, accompanied by the annual book defense and Year 5 exhibition [690].

### 2. Experiential Learning Frameworks & Adult Education [528]
The instructional design of this curriculum is built strictly upon the principles of adult learning (andragogy) and transformative learning:
*   **Kolb's Experiential Learning Cycle**: Every month moves systematically through *Concrete Experience* (making), *Reflective Observation* (critique), *Abstract Conceptualization* (seminar readings), and *Active Experimentation* (materials application) [12, 529].
*   **Schön's Reflective Practitioner**: Studio sessions emphasize "reflection-in-action" (real-time adjustments) and "reflection-on-action" (journaling and logs) to make tacit, maker-based expertise explicit [13, 545, 645].
*   **Knowles' Andragogy**: Prioritizes intrinsic motivation, self-directed exploration, immediate real-world applicability, and the integration of prior knowledge [529, 530, 531].
*   **Universal Design for Learning (UDL)**: Incorporates flexible, accessible pathways that treat physical and cognitive accessibility as a standard rather than an accommodation [13, 223].

### 3. The 10 Bodies of Knowledge (BoKs) [254]
The curriculum comprehensively covers ten integrated subject areas:
1.  **Observational Drawing & Painting Pedagogy**: Transmitting traditional atelier techniques (sight-size, Bargue plates, Nicolaïdes, Zoey Frank) while maintaining individual expressive agency [254].
2.  **Artistic Anatomy & Embodied Cognition**: Structural anatomy (cranium, hands, joints) balanced beside the somatic and interoceptive accounts of the lived body [254].
3.  **Colour & Materials Science**: High-level study of pigment permanence, acrylic polymer chemistry, conservation-grade framing, and spectrometry [254].
4.  **Art History & Criticism of the Figure, Face, and Table**: The historical lineages of portraiture and still-life representations, paired with contemporary figuration discourse (Packer, Toor, Yiadom-Boakye, Saville) [254].
5.  **Practice-Based Research & Research-Creation**: Theoretical and analytical frameworks (Sullivan, Barrett, Bolt, Loveless) that establish a practice as a rigorous academic inquiry [254].
6.  **Critique Pedagogy & Studio Culture**: Constructive, care-based, and feminist critique models that replace traditional, harmful juried spectacles [254].
7.  **The Structure & Economics of the Art World**: Practical navigation of primary and secondary markets, dealer networks, consignment contracts, grants, and CARFAC fees [254].
8.  **Documentation, Archives & Knowledge Mobilization**: Meticulous recording of portfolios using metadata-driven cataloging and digital asset management (DAM) platforms [254].
9.  **Arts in Health, Disability Studies & Ethics of Care**: Establishing the "Commission in Care" as a site of mutual, respectful service where drawing does work in the world [254].
10. **Contemplative Traditions & Theology of the Body**: Exploring the queering-contemplation framework and sacred spaces to express the body's sacramentality [254].

## III. GRADUATION CRITERIA & FIVE-YEAR EVALUATION FRAMEWORK [9, 10]

To measure the effectiveness of this 5-year curriculum without relying on traditional letter grades, you will evaluate your progress quarterly against a nine-level evaluation framework [10], culminating in a Year 5 thesis defense (the *viva*):

1.  **Likeness and likeness-control**: Does the likeness hold across successive months, sittings, and media constraints? Verified by your monthly critic's sentence [714].
2.  **Sittings Log Volume**: Have you maintained a consistent cadence of live sittings? (Target: Minimum 10 sittings logged per year) [696, 703].
3.  **Materials Integrity**: Are all finished panels and papers prepared, sealed, framed, and wired to museum and gallery conservation-grade standard? [254, 701].
4.  **Exhibition Proposals Accepted**: Have you secured organic placement in curated rental and consignment programs or regional artist-run centers? [714, 727].
5.  **Patreon & Public Mobilization**: Have you published monthly process-based instructional entries, translating your technical skills to public audiences? [700, 712].
6.  **The Critic's Sentence**: Does your selected critic verify that your visual vocabulary has significantly shifted, demonstrating growth in your personal voice? [1, 692].
