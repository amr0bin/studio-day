# Andrea Robin Studio — 2026–27

Working files for the four projects I run alongside a day job. Built in the Slow Leaf format.

The hours these are designed around: Sunday 10–6 for making, Monday evening 2–4 hours for writing, documenting, shipping, correspondence and editing.

- `index.html` — the studio day. The landing page, and the one to open on a Sunday morning.
- `studies.html` — the studies series
- `teaching.html` — Foundations, one recorded module a year
- `writing.html` — Dear Ordinary, the craft standard, and the annual book
- `documentation.html` — the capture setup, decided once
- `style.css` — shared styling
- `images/` — photographs

Keep the files and the images folder together; the pages load the stylesheet and photographs by relative path.

Everything settled is written down so it is not decided again. Everything genuinely undecided sits under "Still open" on each page rather than being papered over. Floor costs are midpoint estimates until a full series of actual hours and materials has been tracked.

Pricing structure, tax and deductions, and income modelling live in their own reference files.
