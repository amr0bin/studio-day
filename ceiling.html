The Ceiling2026 to 2036

What the ten years are for

The Inquiry page says what a year does. This says what a decade produces, and what has to be true at the end of each stretch for the next one to be reachable. Written backwards from 2036.

The position

By 2036 this practice should be able to produce a doctoral application, at a practice-based or an art history door, that is competitive for the most selective funding in the country, and a body of work that justifies the decade whether or not that application is ever made.

Both halves carry weight. The second is why the first is possible, because a file assembled in the year it is needed reads as assembly, and a file that accumulated for ten years reads as a life.

Why this is reasonable rather than hopeful

By 2036 the combination is an MSc in disability studies, clinical registration, a decade of accessibility practice inside a university, fifteen years of learning design in health and higher education, a ten-year figurative practice on the body, grief and the sacred, and work delivered inside a care setting with consent and a fee.

The field that wants all of that at once is arts in health and disability arts. It is growing, it is funded, and it is short of people who are credible in the studio and in the clinic at the same time. Most artists entering it hold no clinical or scholarly standing. Most clinicians entering it hold no practice. The career produces the asset rather than competing with it.

Which means the commission, the placed writing, the talks and the named relationships are the parts of the plan that carry the file. The count of paintings a year matters less than whether the work has been delivered in the world and written about afterwards.

What the exceptional file is made of

The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation awards up to fifteen doctoral scholarships a year nationally, at up to $50,000 annually for three years plus up to $20,000 annually for research travel, reaching roughly $210,000. It is for humanities and social sciences candidates whose research relates to one of four themes, the first being Human Rights and Dignity, and it states it looks beyond academic excellence for people who mobilize knowledge, engage in their communities, and are open to non-conventional forms of knowledge. Read 18 September 2026.

Disability justice is theme one. Research-creation is a non-conventional form of knowledge in their own wording. Knowledge mobilization is a decade of learning design and public writing. The part of that file a straight-through candidate cannot fill is the engagement record, because they have been students continuously. This decade produces it by default, provided it is documented as it happens.

Vanier's current criteria and the Sobey age restriction are both unverified as of 18 September 2026.

The five ledgers

Everything the practice does lands in one of five accounts. A year is good when each one gains at least one entry. This is the measure that replaces feeling.

Ledger2036Annual rate
The corpus A named body of work ten years deep, catalogued sheet by sheet, with one definitive large series inside it. Whatever the year allows, catalogued without exception.
The shown record A solo or two-person exhibition at a recognised venue. Work in a public or institutional collection. A national prize finalist listing. One dated showing, escalating in venue. A members’ wall and a public gallery are the same action at different altitudes.
The funded record Municipal, then provincial, then federal. An Explore and Create grant held. A commission delivered at CARFAC rates inside a care setting. Three applications, minimum, regardless of outcome. Grant records compound.
The scholarly record Four to six citable items. One reading language. Art history coursework. A master’s carrying affiliation. One placed piece every twelve to eighteen months.
The relational record A supervisor who has known the work for years. A critic of a decade. Referees who write specifically. Curators, a health authority partner, a mentor. Two new named relationships, each kept warm with two contacts a year.

The relational ledger is the only one that cannot be produced in the year it is needed, and it is the one most applicants are thinnest in.

Three things that make this file exceptional rather than complete

The delivered care commission. Almost no doctoral applicant in a fine arts department has produced funded work with a health authority, under consent, with a fee paid and a public talk given. It evidences research ethics literacy, knowledge mobilization and community engagement at once, which are three separate criteria on most major fellowship forms.

Dual standing. A registered clinician with a decade-long exhibiting practice can make claims about the body, care and attention that neither an artist nor a clinician alone can make, and can be believed on both. That is an argument for the research question rather than a biography.

The dated record. A catalogue and a writing archive under version control, running from 2026, is an unusual object. It demonstrates sustained independent research across a period no coursework could cover, and it is checkable.

Asymmetric moves, cheapest now

Target the institution, not only the role. UBC’s tuition waiver gives eligible staff, including Management and Professional groups, twelve credits of undergraduate courses, or twelve credits of graduate courses, or graduate program fees up to the normal program fee, in every twelve-month period, and states the courses need not relate to the current role. Read 18 September 2026. That makes an employer worth a master’s degree and an affiliation. SFU’s equivalent is unverified.

The collaborator route. SSHRC’s terminology states collaborators may contribute to the intellectual direction and conduct of research and do not need to be affiliated with a Canadian postsecondary institution. One named collaborator role on a funded arts-in-health project puts tri-agency research on the CV years before a doctorate. It is a relationship rather than an application.

Start the citable record before it is needed. One exposition or article in 2027 changes how every subsequent application reads, and it costs writing that is already happening.

Write the question down annually. One paragraph, revised each year, on what the practice is finding out. Ten dated versions of that paragraph are a research trajectory. It is the cheapest item here and the one every door reads.

How this relates to the standing order

The standing order on the front page is the minimum rate at which the five ledgers keep accumulating through a degree year, a job change or a practicum. This page is what they accumulate toward. The two are the same plan at two intensities, and neither is the ambitious one.

The test for any year, asked once in November: did each of the five ledgers gain at least one entry? A year where all five moved is a good year, whatever it felt like. Nothing here requires a heroic month.

Still to verify: SFU’s staff tuition benefit; whether staff at either university can be named collaborators on tri-agency grants; Vanier and Sobey terms; Canada Council Deaf and Disability Arts and Access Support eligibility; whether the Emily Carr-era exhibitions paid CARFAC fees; which Vancouver health authorities commission, and on what terms; and the Journal for Artistic Research submission terms for unaffiliated practitioners.
