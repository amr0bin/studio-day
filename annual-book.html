The annual bookWorking file

A year gathered, and then set down

Each year I bring the writing, the process, and the work into one volume. It closes the chapter.

Nothing in it is made for it. Each stream is produced in the ordinary run of the year and gathered at the end. That is what keeps the book an act of ending rather than another project competing for the same hours.

The book itself

Purpose
To close the chapterThe book is the act of ending a year of work, not a product built to sell. Anything else it does is secondary.
Contents
Writing, process, worksIn that order or interleaved — a decision each year makes for itself, depending on what the year held.
Completion
November, annuallyNot negotiable. It protects December.
Where it happens
Monday evenings, September to NovemberCompilation is assembly, not making. It never takes studio Sundays.
Continuity
Same trim, same typography, same spineThe volumes should read as a set on a shelf. Fixing this once removes an annual decision.

December

December is a retreat. It holds my birthday and the anniversary of a significant loss in my family, and I keep it free of obligation to anyone but myself wherever I can.

The November deadline exists so this is genuinely possible. Nothing launches in December. Nothing ships in December. Anything already recorded continues to sell on its own, and nothing requires me.

This runs against the commercial grain, since December is a strong month for selling art. The decision holds regardless.
