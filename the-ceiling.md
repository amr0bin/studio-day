# The Ceiling

*Working note, 18 September 2026. The companion to `the-floor.md`. The floor is the minimum rate of accumulation. This is what the rate is for. Written backwards from 2036, on the assumption that the practice is taken as seriously as the evidence says it deserves.*

---

## The position, stated once

**By 2036 the practice should be able to produce a doctoral application, at a practice-based or art history door, that is competitive for the most selective funding in the country, and a body of work that would justify the decade whether or not that application is ever made.**

Both halves are load-bearing. The second one is why the first is achievable, because a file assembled in the year it is needed reads as assembly, and a file that accumulated for ten years reads as a life.

---

## Why this is a reasonable target rather than a hopeful one

The decade produces a combination that has very little competition in Canada.

By 2036: an MSc in disability studies, clinical registration, a decade of accessibility practice inside a university, fifteen years of learning design in health and higher education, a ten-year figurative practice on the body, grief and the sacred, and work delivered inside a care setting with consent and a fee.

The field that wants all of that at once is arts in health and disability arts. It is growing, it is funded, and it is short of people who are credible in the studio and in the clinic at the same time. Most artists entering it have no clinical or scholarly standing. Most clinicians entering it have no practice. The combination is the asset, and it is produced by the career rather than despite it.

Two consequences follow. The career stops being the thing the studio is squeezed around. And the parts of the plan that looked like extras — the commission, the placed writing, the talks, the relationships — become the parts that carry the file.

## What the exceptional file is made of

The Pierre Elliott Trudeau Foundation awards up to fifteen doctoral scholarships a year nationally, at up to $50,000 annually for three years plus up to $20,000 annually for research travel and networking, reaching roughly $210,000. It is for humanities and social sciences doctoral candidates whose research relates to one of four themes, the first being Human Rights and Dignity. The Foundation states it looks beyond academic excellence for candidates who can mobilize knowledge, engage actively in their communities and contribute to public dialogue, and describes shaping researchers who are open to non-conventional forms of knowledge. Verified 18 September 2026 from the Foundation's own pages and UBC's award listing.

Read against that: disability justice is theme one. Research-creation is a non-conventional form of knowledge in their own wording. Knowledge mobilization is a decade of learning design and public writing. Engaged leadership is the private practice, the accessibility work and the care commission.

The part of a Trudeau file that straight-through candidates cannot fill is the engagement and leadership record, because they have been students continuously. That is the part this decade produces by default, provided it is documented as it happens.

**Verify:** Vanier's current criteria, value and nomination process, and whether the Sobey Art Award still carries an age restriction. Both marked unverified as of 18 September 2026.

## The five ledgers

Everything the practice does lands in one of five accounts. A year is good when each account gains at least one entry. This is the measure that replaces feeling.

### 1 · The corpus

**2036 target.** A named body of work ten years deep, catalogued sheet by sheet from the first entry, with one definitive large series inside it and a public exhibition that reads as a direction rather than a selection.

**Annual rate.** Whatever the year allows, catalogued without exception. The catalogue is the only account that cannot be reconstructed after the fact, and its dated version history in the repository is itself evidence of sustained independent research.

### 2 · The shown record

**2036 target.** A solo or two-person exhibition at a recognised venue. Work in at least one public, institutional or corporate collection. A national prize finalist listing. A run of juried group shows with fees paid.

**Annual rate.** One dated showing, escalating in venue over the decade. A members' wall in 2027 and a public gallery in 2034 are the same annual action at different altitudes.

### 3 · The funded record

**2036 target.** The ladder complete: municipal, then provincial, then federal. A Canada Council Explore and Create grant held. A commission delivered at CARFAC rates inside a care setting. Ideally, a named collaborator role on a tri-agency grant held by someone else.

**Annual rate.** Three applications, minimum, regardless of outcome. Grant records compound, because each success changes how the next application reads.

### 4 · The scholarly record

**2036 target.** Four to six citable items: an exposition in the *Journal for Artistic Research* or its Research Catalogue, an article in the *Canadian Journal of Disability Studies* or similar, a catalogue essay, conference papers. One reading language. Art history coursework. A master's carrying affiliation.

**Annual rate.** One placed piece every twelve to eighteen months. SSHRC's definition of a record of research achievement covers any identifiable contribution to the advancement, development and mobilization of knowledge, which is broader than most artists assume and includes public scholarship. SFU's doctoral application accepts catalogue essays, published articles and grant applications as the academic writing sample.

### 5 · The relational record

**2036 target.** A supervisor who has known the work for years. A critic of a decade's standing. Referees who can write specifically rather than warmly. Curators inside the disability arts network. A health authority partner. A mentor.

**Annual rate.** Two new named relationships, kept warm with two contacts a year each. This is the only account that cannot be produced in the year it is needed, and it is the one most applicants are thinnest in.

## The decade in five movements

### 2026–2027 · Restart, and be seen

The hand back, the record started, the first jury.

**True at the end:** the critic exists by name. Twelve current record shots exist. The catalogue is running with no uncatalogued work. One juried entry submitted. The Canada Council and BC Arts Council profiles validated. The exhibition-fee question answered, which decides whether provincial money is open now.

### 2027–2029 · Affiliation, and the first citable piece

Leeds begins. The Accessibility Advisor role is the strategic target, for the affiliation and the tuition waiver more than the salary.

**True at the end:** the MSc underway. One placed piece of writing in a citable venue. First grant application submitted. The staff tuition benefit confirmed at whichever institution employs you, and the first art history course or language course taken on it. One named relationship inside a university art department.

### 2029–2031 · The commission, and the body of work

The work goes into the world, with consent, at a fee.

**True at the end:** a commission delivered inside a care setting, budgeted at CARFAC rates, with a public talk in their room. A solo or two-person exhibition proposed in writing and shown. Art history credits and the reading language accumulating on the waiver. A second placed piece.

### 2031–2033 · The master's, and the claim

Affiliation arrives with the degree, and with it the first real access to tri-agency money.

**True at the end:** a master's underway with an affiliation attached. A peer-reviewed piece stating what the practice has found, in its own terms. The corpus at twenty or more works under one thesis. A supervisor relationship at the intended doctoral school that predates the application by years.

### 2033–2036 · The file, and the application

**True at the end:** the doctoral application submitted with a portfolio, a research question a decade old, an academic writing sample from a real journal, and referees who have watched the work. The funding file assembled in parallel: SSHRC doctoral, and Trudeau or Vanier where eligible. An exhibition that reads as a direction.

## The three things that make this file exceptional rather than merely complete

1. **The delivered care commission.** Almost no doctoral applicant in a fine arts department has produced funded work with a health authority, under consent, with a fee paid and a public talk given. It evidences research ethics literacy, knowledge mobilization and community engagement at once, which are three separate criteria on most major fellowship forms.

2. **Dual standing.** A registered clinician with a decade-long exhibiting practice can make claims about the body, care and attention that neither an artist nor a clinician alone can make, and can be believed on both. This is the argument for the research question, not merely a biography.

3. **The decade-long dated record.** A catalogue and a writing archive under version control, running from 2026, is an unusual object. It demonstrates sustained independent research over a period no coursework could cover, and it is checkable.

## Asymmetric moves, cheapest now

**Target the institution, not only the role.** UBC's tuition waiver gives eligible staff, including Management and Professional groups, 12 credits of undergraduate courses, or 12 credits of graduate courses, or graduate program fees up to the normal program fee, in every 12-month period, and states the courses need not relate to the current role. Verified 18 September 2026. That makes an employer worth a master's degree and an affiliation. **Verify SFU's equivalent before assuming parity.**

**The collaborator route.** SSHRC's terminology states collaborators may contribute to the intellectual direction and conduct of research and do not need to be affiliated with a Canadian postsecondary institution. One named collaborator role on a funded arts-in-health or research-creation project puts tri-agency research on the CV years before a doctorate. This is a relationship rather than an application, and working inside a university is how it becomes available.

**Start the citable record before it is needed.** One exposition or article in 2027 changes how every subsequent application reads, and it costs writing that is already happening.

**Write the question down annually.** One paragraph, revised each year, on what the practice is finding out. Ten dated versions of that paragraph are a research trajectory. It is the single cheapest item here and the one every door reads.

---

## How this relates to the floor

The floor is the minimum rate at which the five ledgers keep accumulating through a degree year, a job change or a practicum. The ceiling is what they accumulate toward.

The test for any year, asked once in November: **did each of the five ledgers gain at least one entry?** A year where all five moved is a good year, whatever it felt like. A year where three moved is still a year on the trajectory. Nothing here requires a heroic month.
