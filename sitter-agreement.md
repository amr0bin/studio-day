## What is going to happen

I am going to draw or paint you from life. The sitting will last about ____ hours. You can stop at any point, for any reason, without explaining why, and nothing will be owed either way.

## What is being made

One or more drawings or paintings. I decide what the work looks like and whether it is finished. If it does not work, it goes in the file or in the bin, and that is not a comment on you.

## What you get

A photographic record print of the finished work, free, whether or not the work is ever shown.

## Who owns what

I own the artwork itself and the copyright in it, as the person who made it. You keep every right you have in your own likeness, which is why the next section exists and is separate.
