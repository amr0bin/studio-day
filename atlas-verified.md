# Atlas verification record

Entries read on the organisation's own site, with the date. Distances are from Thurlow and Pendrell. Anything not listed here is still unverified and does not go on the Atlas.

## Vancouver batch, 5 September 2026

**Basic Inquiry / The Vancouver Life Drawing Society** — 1011 Main Street, Vancouver. Volunteer-run registered charity, founded 1983; several uninstructed figure sessions a week from the live model; members exhibit in the studio gallery; tickets and passes through the Robostrar booking system, cheaper as a member. Test passed: (c) sustained access to the model near home. Source: https://lifedrawing.org/ (getting-here, book-a-session pages). Verified 2026-09-05. From the West End: Skytrain to Main Street–Science World, walk north.

**Kroma Artist's Acrylics** — Net Loft, 1649 Duranleau Street, Granville Island. Small-batch acrylics, mediums and gesso made on site since 1970; sold only direct, from the workshop, by phone or online; Kevin Head, Emily Carr diploma, has run it since 1993. Test passed: materials relationship; the sponsorship conversation is with the maker, not a distributor. Source: https://kromaacrylics.com/pages/about-kroma and /pages/contact. Verified 2026-09-05. From the West End: Aquabus from the foot of Hornby, or the 50 bus.

**Arts Umbrella** — 1400 Johnston Street, Granville Island (also Surrey Centre). Non-profit arts education for ages 2 to 22; ten visual arts studios on Granville Island; charitable registration 118852300 RR 0001; third-party fundraising events are welcomed and supported with donation forms, program information and signage, contact specialevents@artsumbrella.com; Splash is the annual donated-art auction. Test passed: the giving relationship, and a possible sponsor for a City of Vancouver CASC application. Source: https://www.artsumbrella.com/support/fundraising-events/ and /granville-island/. Verified 2026-09-05.

**Opus Art Supplies** — Downtown store on Granville Street; Granville Island store on Johnston Street. Free membership with cashback; in-store and online member events and demos; formal partnerships with the Federation of Canadian Artists and Emily Carr; a demo-artist application and a collaborator program exist on the site. Test passed: the workshop conversation has a named route (Demo Artist application, events page). Source: https://opusartsupplies.com (site navigation read on the paper product page). Verified 2026-09-05. Store addresses and the demo-artist terms still to be read on their own pages.

**City of Vancouver cultural grants** — CASC is for organisations; individuals from equity-denied communities apply through a sponsor. 2027 intake closes 7 October 2026. Recorded on the Calendar. Source: https://vancouver.ca/people-programs/cultural-grants-program.aspx. Verified 2026-09-05.
