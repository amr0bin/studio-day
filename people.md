# People

*The relational ledger. The only one of the five that cannot be built in the year it is needed, and the one most applicants are thinnest in.*

**The rate:** two new named relationships a year, each kept warm with two contacts a year. Contact means something specific — a piece of work sent, a question asked, a show attended and mentioned. A newsletter is not a contact.

**The rule:** nobody is asked for something the first time they are written to.
