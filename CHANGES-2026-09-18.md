## One line on `writing.html` worth revising

It currently reads that SSHRC research-creation funding requires affiliation
with an eligible Canadian institution, "which is the concrete reason a future
MFA or PhD affiliation is what would unlock funded studio time." That is true
and incomplete. SSHRC's terminology page also states that collaborators may
contribute to the intellectual direction and conduct of research and do not need
to be affiliated with a Canadian postsecondary institution. A staff post at a
university is therefore a route to tri-agency research on the CV years before a
doctorate, and the sentence should say so.

## The one substantive thing I did not do

`calendar.html` was not extended to 2036 with dated entries, and should not be.
The page's own rule is that dates are read on the organisation's page and stamped
with the day they were read. Projecting deadlines eight years out would break
that rule and put stale figures somewhere they would get trusted.

What went in instead is the ladder: the order the rungs are climbed in, with no
dates, plus the rule that the dated window runs about eighteen months and is
rewritten each November. The order is the part that is knowable now, and it is
the part that actually governs a decision.

## The verification list, gathered from every page

Each of these is marked unverified somewhere in the repository. They are listed
together so the autumn's emails can be written in one sitting.

1. SFU's staff tuition benefit terms, against UBC's.
2. Whether staff at either university hold tri-agency eligible-applicant status,
   or can be named collaborators on a funded grant.
3. SFU SCA, SIAT, UBC AHVA and UVic AHVS: admission with a BFA plus a
   professional record, and what each counts as equivalent. Ask the graduate
   secretary; do not read the calendar alone.
4. Vanier criteria and value; whether the Sobey Art Award still carries an age
   restriction; whether the Elizabeth Greenshields window is open.
5. Canada Council Deaf and Disability Arts stream and Access Support eligibility.
6. Whether the Emily Carr-era exhibitions paid CARFAC fees, which decides
   whether BC Arts Council is open now or in three years.
7. Which Vancouver health authorities and foundations commission artists, and on
   what terms.
8. *Journal for Artistic Research* submission terms for unaffiliated
   practitioners.
9. Which art history courses can be taken part-time or by distance, and whether
   the BFA carries any toward the count.

## What the repository now says that it did not say this morning

Three positions, each stated in one place and cross-referenced from the rest.

**Study is a decision, not a hope.** Two pages carried the old sentence that
further study in fine art "is not an option at present." Both are rewritten, and
both now carry a date.

**The practice is an inquiry, not a curriculum.** The training scaffold is
unchanged and still good. What changed is what it is called in public, and why,
stated with the SSHRC clause beside it.

**The measure is accumulation, not survival.** Five ledgers, one question every
November, and a rate that works the same in a degree year and a clear one.
