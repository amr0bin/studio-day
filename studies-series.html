One hour, and the face before it settles

The studies are where I meet the subject weekly. One size, one price, one premise, an hour a piece, numbered from the first.

The constraints aren't a limit placed on the work. They are what lets the work happen at all inside the hours I have.

The studio day

Sunday, ten until six. Six pieces. Select a block to see what it holds.

The month

Making happens on Sundays. Everything else happens on Monday evenings.

What stays fixed

Every variable left open costs a decision later. These are settled, and I don't reopen them piece by piece.

Format
8 × 10 inches, archival paperOne size only. It fits the hour, ships flat, and is small enough that someone can own five.
Medium
Pencil, and alla prima in acrylicBoth reach finished quality inside the hour without frustration.
Subject
The body and the portrait as natureOne premise, varied endlessly within it.
Price
$150Flat, no negotiation, no sale prices. Accessibility here is a choice, not a concession.
Numbering
Sequential from 001, permanentIt marks an ongoing practice rather than inventory, and makes milestones legible.
Rhythm
One release a month, fixed day and hourFirst come, first served. Work made after a release begins the next month's group.
Donation
$25 of every sale to Arts UmbrellaCountable, easy to state, easy to report.

My floor per study is roughly $62 once materials, the hour, and a share of overhead are counted. Shipping is charged to the buyer at cost. The finishing kit — a glassine envelope, an acid-free backing board, a rigid mailer, a handwritten card — accounts for most of the per-piece cost.
