---

## Conventions, decided once

- **Reverse chronological** within every section. Most recent first, always.
- **One line per entry**, in this order: year, title in italics, venue, city, and where relevant the type in brackets. `2027. *Painting on the Edge.* Federation Gallery, Vancouver. (Juried)`
- **Solo and group are separate sections.** Merging them reads as padding.
- **Mark what was juried, curated, or invited.** Juries ask; it is the difference between a wall and a record.
- **Mark which exhibitions paid a fee.** BC Arts Council asks for two curated exhibitions with fees paid, and this is the field that decides whether provincial money is open now. Carried on the Calendar's readiness kit as an open question.
- **Never claim an affiliation that does not exist.** Working at Emily Carr is employment; it is not endorsement of the practice, and it goes under Professional Experience rather than anywhere else.
- **Three pages and one page.** The one-page version keeps education, selected exhibitions, awards, and collections, and drops everything else. Both live in this file so they cannot drift apart.
- **The ORCID iD goes in the header** once it exists. Almost no artist CV carries one, and every academic reader knows what it is.

# What I need from you

Send these as a plain list, in any order, and I will set the whole thing.

1. **Emily Carr.** Years of the BFA, from entry to graduation. The degree title as awarded. The name of the entrance scholarship, if it had one.
2. **The group shows during the degree.** Year, title, venue, and whether each was juried, curated or open. Anything you remember; approximate titles are better than omissions, and I will mark anything uncertain.
3. **The awards and scholarships.** Exact names, awarding bodies, years, and values where they were cash.
4. **The international study.** Institution, city, country, year, and what form it took.
5. **The seven years at Emily Carr.** Three roles, their titles, and the years of each.
6. **Dalhousie.** Programme name, years, whether completed, and the credential.
7. **Exhibition fees.** Whether any of the Emily Carr-era shows paid a fee. If you do not know, say so and it becomes a question for the registrar or the organisers.
8. **Anything acquired.** Whether any work went into a collection, private or institutional.

Two notes on this. Approximate is fine and I will mark it; invented is not, and I will not fill a gap with something plausible. And a record that looks thin in 2026 is a record that started, which is the only thing that matters about it.
